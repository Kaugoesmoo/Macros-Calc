How to use this responsibly

The calorie target is an input—not a guarantee of a deficit. Estimate maintenance calories, choose a suitable deficit, and review your average weight trend over 2–3 weeks alongside training performance, recovery and hunger.

This calculator is for general nutrition planning and does not replace individual medical or dietetic advice.
